# Pegasus Airlines

**Subtitle:** Low cost to fly. High standards for design.

**Role:** Creative Director
**Year:** 2017

---

**Description**

Pegasus Airlines is a low-cost carrier based in Turkey operating routes across Europe and beyond. I worked on two parallel projects: the corporate website and the in-flight menu program for both domestic and international routes.

On the website, I ran stakeholder workshops, card sorting sessions, and wireframing before moving into design and A/B testing. We built, maintained, and updated the site over two years. On the menu side, I worked as art director alongside food photographers and food stylists, collaborating closely with the in-flight service team to produce menus that worked across both domestic and international flights.
