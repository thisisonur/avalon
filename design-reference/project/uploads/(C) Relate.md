# Relate

**Subtitle:** Building trust from the ground up

**Role:** Brand Designer, Design System Designer, Product Designer
**Year:** 2025

---

**Description**

Relate is an AI platform that measures trust in every conversation. It helps teams understand what's actually happening in their meetings, not just what was said.

I worked across the full project: brand identity, marketing website, design system, and every product screen. The mark is a handshake viewed from above, abstracted into something modern. Blue and pink for each person, a gradient where they meet. I layered the design system on top of HASTE, built out the components with accessibility as the foundation, and documented everything so designers and engineers worked from the same source.
