# Off the Clock

Off the clock, I make ice cream in flavors that confuse people (black pepper, mostly), roll pasta dough by hand, and chase whatever recipe sits between traditional and slightly unhinged. Weekends belong to Billie, our rescue pit bull, the sweetest dog alive and still a work in progress on some things. And I've been playing Diablo since it was pixelated on a slow PC. My Xbox wrap-up tells on me every year.
