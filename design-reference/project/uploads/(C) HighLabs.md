# HighLabs

**Subtitle:** Clean enough to trust. Warm enough to smile.

**Role:** Brand Designer
**Year:** 2023

---

**Description**

HighLabs is a medical cannabis provider based in Sydney. It's a category that tends toward either the clinical or the countercultural. Neither felt right for where they wanted the brand to go.

After a discovery workshop with the founding team, I developed an identity that entirely stepped away from the category's visual tropes. The system covers logo, color, typography, and brand language. Clean and minimal, but with enough warmth to feel human. The result is a brand that earns trust without feeling cold and shows personality without losing credibility.
