# LiveTiles Reach

**Subtitle:** Putting content where people actually look

**Role:** Product Designer, Design System Designer
**Year:** 2021

---

**Description**

LiveTiles Reach is an employee communications platform for web and mobile. The core strategy was clear: make content the center of the experience, not something users have to go looking for.

I worked across three areas: onboarding, the daily content experience, and the dashboard. I started by establishing the design system foundations, rebuilt the component library, and ran user testing on onboarding flows. From there, I redesigned the dashboard to put content front and center. Throughout, I worked alongside engineers in Storybook to make sure what shipped matched what was designed.
