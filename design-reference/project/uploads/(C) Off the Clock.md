# Off the Clock

I cook with the same obsessive attention I bring to token naming. Working through technique, learning from failure, occasionally getting something right. The process is where the satisfaction lives.

Weekends are for wandering the city with my partner and our rescue dog Billie. We end up at beer gardens more than anywhere else. I do yoga, hit the gym, and I'm in a slow-burn negotiation with an assisted pullup bar about upper body strength. Progress is measured in inches, not miles.

I sketch when I need to think without structure. I've been playing Diablo since it was pixelated on a slow PC. Some things stick with you. That's just how it is.
