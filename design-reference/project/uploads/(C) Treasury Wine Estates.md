# Treasury Wine Estates: Multi-Brand Design System

**4 min read**

A tokenized design system that enabled twelve websites from one shared foundation, with each wine brand maintaining its own distinct identity.

---

## My Role

Design System Designer
2023
**Duration:** 6 months

---

## The Situation

Treasury Wine Estates owns many wine brands, including Lindeman's, Matua, and Pepperjack. The company wanted to launch twelve websites (eleven for individual brands plus a global site) within six months. But each brand needed its own look and feel, while also sharing the same basic components and features.

The challenge was tough:
- Each brand needed its own unique identity and design
- Building everything separately would take too long
- Managing twelve different systems would be impossible to maintain

We needed a system that could support each brand's unique identity while keeping everything consistent underneath.

---

## What I Did

I designed a shared design system that allowed each brand to stay unique while reusing the same components and patterns.

The key idea: **One system, many brands.** I created 120+ components and 32+ layouts without any brand colors or styles built in. Think of it like a foundation. It's the same for everyone, but each brand adds their own colors, fonts, and spacing on top.

I also:
- Created brand-specific token sets (colors, fonts, spacing) that each brand could customize
- Worked with designers and engineers to test everything across all twelve websites
- Ran QA across components in Storybook and Chromatic to validate consistency and catch visual regressions
- Made sure all components met accessibility standards
- Validated the system worked for real content and user scenarios across all brands

---

## Results

The project delivered all eleven brand websites plus the global Treasury Wine Estates website within six months. But the real benefit was long-term. Marketing and digital teams now have one system to manage instead of twelve separate ones.

The system helped:
- Launch twelve websites on time
- Reduce design and development time
- Give each brand complete design freedom
- Make it easy to launch, update, and manage brands efficiently

The system is now used by marketing and digital teams to evolve and launch new brand sites without rebuilding components from scratch.

---

**Key takeaway:** The best systems get out of the way. They let each brand shine while saving everyone time in the background.
