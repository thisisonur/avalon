# About

**I build design systems that know what the brand is supposed to feel like.**

I've worked for 20+ years across brand, product, and UI design. The common thread has always been translation, turning what a brand means into how a product looks and behaves. That work taught me something: brand isn't a layer you add at the end. It's the framework that every other decision comes from.

I've also built brands from scratch, naming, identity, voice. So when I design a system, I know what brand intent looks like before it becomes a token. The tokens aren't just variables. They carry that intent into the product.

Today I focus on multi-brand and AI-product systems, where the brand-to-system translation matters most. Architectures that match their operational context, scoped to the team that has to maintain them, designed to let each brand stay itself even as the system holds them together.
