# How I Work

**Brand intent, encoded in tokens**
Most systems treat brand as a surface layer. I work from brand first. Brand is the decision-making framework. I translate brand intent into token architecture and naming conventions so every choice designers and engineers make is brand-aligned, not a guess.

**Architecture first, aesthetics later**
Structure before style. I start by understanding the scope: how many brands, how many platforms, what engineering needs. I design the architecture first, then apply aesthetics. The foundation determines what's possible. Beauty follows structure, never the other way around.

**Engineering is design, design is engineering**
I build with the code, not separate from it. Documentation, component validation, and real implementation scenarios are the work. Engineers see problems designers miss. Designers see possibilities that engineers haven't considered. This collaboration is where systems actually get built.

**Standards enable scale**
Documentation, naming conventions, token structure, and accessibility. Good standards get out of the way and let teams move fast. I prioritize documentation as it communicates intent to designers, engineers, and AI agents. AI outcomes depend on the robustness of documentation and how gaps are addressed.
