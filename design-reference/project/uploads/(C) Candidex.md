# Candidex

**Subtitle:** The limitless imagination of a child

**Role:** Brand Designer
**Year:** 2025

---

**Description**

Candidex is a recruitment company in New Zealand focused on early childhood education. They needed a new identity that felt playful and purposeful without tipping into the generic. The brief was clear; the execution had to match the ambition.

I built the mark around the Tangram puzzle, a fixed set of seven shapes that combine into almost endless forms. The form is a flying figure, a nod to the educators Candidex serves. People moving to a new country, starting a new chapter. The mark carries that sense of movement and possibility without spelling it out.
