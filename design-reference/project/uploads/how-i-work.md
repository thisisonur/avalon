# How I Work

**Brand intent, encoded into the system**
Most systems treat brand as a surface layer. I work the other way around. Brand is the decision-making framework, and I translate that intent into token architecture so every choice is brand-aligned by default, not a guess.

**Structure shapes what's possible**
Foundations before surfaces. I scope the architecture to the project's reality: how many brands, how many platforms, and how the system will be maintained over time. Structure determines what the system can express later. Visual style sits on top, never the other way around.

**Designed for the people who build with it**
A design system only works if the team can actually use it. Adoption is part of the design problem, not a thing that comes after. Documentation, naming conventions, training, the operational model itself, all of it gets scoped together. The system gets built in the overlap between designers and engineers, and it lives or dies based on whether the people using it can move with confidence.

**Documentation is the interface**
Naming conventions, token structure, accessibility, decision rationale, these are what let teams move fast without breaking things. Good documentation isn't a wrapper around the system; it's part of the system. And it isn't just for humans anymore. It's the interface between your system and every AI agent that will touch it.

**Express intent with less**
I design for restraint, especially in domains where users are stressed, where trust is the product, where clarity is more valuable than personality. The AI category in particular pulls toward visual excess, and I work the other way. The brand can carry the energy. The interface should carry the work.
