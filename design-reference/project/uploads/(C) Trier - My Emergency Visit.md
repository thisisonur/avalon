# Trier: Emergency Department Triage Platform

**4 min read**

A digital triage platform for Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital in Perth, Australia that reduced ambulance wait times by 50% by moving patient intake to before arrival.

---

## My Role

Product Designer and Design System Designer
2023
**Duration:** 9 months

---

## The Situation

Emergency departments are always busy and stressful. Patients fill out paper forms in waiting areas, sharing sensitive information in public. Then staff have to enter that same information into different systems, again and again. This takes time and creates mistakes.

The problems were clear:
- Patients had to fill out forms on paper in the waiting room (no privacy)
- Staff entered the same information multiple times into different systems
- Paper records were hard to access and share quickly
- Busy periods made everything slower and more chaotic

We needed a better way for patients to share information and for staff to access it quickly.

---

## What I Did

I worked with doctors, nurses, and other staff at Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital to understand exactly how they worked and what they needed. Then I designed a digital platform that lets patients start the triage process before they even arrive at the hospital.

The key idea: **Get information earlier, work faster.** Patients use a mobile app to securely enter their information before arriving at the hospital. When they get to the emergency department, their information is already there. Staff don't have to ask the same questions again.

The platform:
- Lets patients enter health information securely through a mobile app
- Organizes all patient information in one place (instead of scattered across different systems)
- Helps staff track patient observations and conditions in real time
- Reduces the need to re-enter information
- Keeps patient information private and secure in a busy environment

I also:
- Worked closely with clinical staff to test and improve the design
- Made sure the platform fit into how staff actually work
- Designed clear communication about wait times to reduce patient anxiety
- Created a design system to keep everything consistent and easy to update

---

## Results

The platform significantly improved how the emergency department worked. When tested at Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital, it achieved a **50% reduction in ambulance ramping** (the time ambulances had to wait outside because the emergency department was full).

The platform helped:
- Get patient information faster
- Reduce unnecessary repetition for staff
- Improve decision-making during busy times
- Reduce patient anxiety through clear communication
- Free up staff time to focus on actual patient care

---

**Key takeaway:** Sometimes the biggest impact comes from fixing how information flows. Better data, faster decisions, less stress for everyone.
