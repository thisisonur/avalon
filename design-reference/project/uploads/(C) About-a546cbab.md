# About

I build design systems that know what the brand is supposed to feel like.

I've spent 20+ years moving between brand, product, and digital work. That work taught me how to translate brand intent into systems architecture. Brand isn't a layer you add. It's the decision-making framework.

Some of that work started from zero. Naming, identity, voice, the whole foundation. I've built brands from scratch. The tokens aren't just variables. They carry intent.

Today I focus on tokenized design systems for multi-brand and multi-platform environments. Built close to engineering, anchored in brand intent, designed to scale without losing identity.
